# Dr.Vas AI Evaluation & Training Workbench

Updated: 5 November 2026
Status: product direction only; no evaluator service, customer data pipeline, marketplace, billing or public offer has been launched.

## Product placement

Build this capability inside the existing Dr.Vas Studio and Dr.Vas Business workspaces, not as a seventh public workspace.

## Intended service areas

1. **AI response evaluation:** correctness, relevance, completeness, clarity, instruction-following and evidence/source quality.
2. **Human review and quality assurance:** trained or domain-qualified reviewers, double review for high-impact work, adjudication and measurable inter-rater agreement.
3. **Prompt and workflow evaluation:** compare prompts and models against versioned test sets and repeatable regression benchmarks.
4. **Dataset preparation and annotation:** classification, tagging, ranking, transcription review and structured feedback, only where source data is lawfully usable.
5. **AI training support:** create licensed, documented examples and feedback datasets; improve task instructions and rubrics; support fine-tuning or preference-data preparation only where provider terms and customer permissions allow it.
6. **Business services:** evaluation reports, quality dashboards, audit trails and managed evaluation projects for companies.

## Potential revenue models to validate

- Paid evaluation projects.
- Per-task or per-item human review.
- Monthly business subscriptions.
- Custom benchmark packages.
- Managed quality-assurance contracts.

Do not publish prices or promise margins until pilot demand, reviewer rates, provider costs and operating expenses are measured.

## Differentiation

Use transparent scoring rubrics, traceable evidence, calibrated human reviewers, repeatable benchmarks, privacy-by-design, domain-specific quality controls and clear confidence/limitations. Do not claim certification or guaranteed AI improvement without independent evidence.

## Safety, rights and quality gates

- Never use private customer data, personal data, copyrighted material or confidential business data for model training without documented lawful basis, appropriate permissions/licences, contractual authority and retention/deletion controls.
- Separate evaluation from training: submitting content for evaluation must not automatically grant permission to use it for training.
- Minimise and redact personal/sensitive data; restrict access by role; record dataset provenance, licence/consent, reviewer actions and deletion status.
- Health, legal, finance and other high-impact evaluations require domain-qualified reviewers and appropriate professional/regulatory review. Automated scoring alone is insufficient.
- Build human-in-the-loop review, reviewer calibration, disagreement handling, sampling audits, bias checks, appeal/correction paths and versioned rubrics.
- Do not promise jobs, guaranteed income, certification, model improvement or customer outcomes until supported by actual contracts and measured results.

## Build sequence

1. Define evaluation schema, rubrics and workflow.
2. Build a private prototype using synthetic or public-domain/licensed sample data.
3. Add repeatable rubric scoring, human review, benchmark runs, version history and exportable reports.
4. Validate reviewer calibration, privacy controls, provenance and deletion.
5. Pilot with a small number of real customers under written terms.
6. Consider a vetted reviewer network only after the workflows and customer demand are proven.
